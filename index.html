NEW PRODUCT • COMMUNITY SAFETY

Community Gadget

A community emergency-alert concept beginning as a mobile app and later expanding to smartwatches. A member who feels threatened can trigger an alert that shares their precise location with verified people in their community, helping nearby members respond quickly while official emergency services remain essential.

Created from an idea by Mawela Nkoriso, the newest member of Cyber Pulse and the mind behind Community Gadget.
