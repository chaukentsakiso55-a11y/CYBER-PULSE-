Cyber Pulse is a youth-led technology initiative exploring artificial intelligence, education, student productivity and digital safety—turning ambitious ideas into practical prototypes and products.

Explore our projectsMeet the team
Our mission

Build technology that solves real problems for students and communities, while giving young innovators room to research, design, experiment and create.

What we build

Cyber Pulse projects

Our portfolio spans focused student tools, digital-safety concepts and larger experimental AI platforms. Projects may be at different stages of research and development.

Education + Focus

StudyLock

A student-focused study and distraction-management platform designed around focus sessions, AI tutoring, study controls and family-supported learning.

Digital Safety

Guardian AI

A safety-focused AI concept aimed at helping protect young users from harmful online experiences, including inappropriate content, phishing and cyberbullying.

Artificial Intelligence

Star AI

An AI project within Cyber Pulse exploring intelligent assistance and new ways for people to interact with useful AI systems.

Experimental Platform

Infinity OS

An ambitious experimental AI environment exploring multi-model intelligence, automation, voice, device interaction and offline/online AI workflows.
