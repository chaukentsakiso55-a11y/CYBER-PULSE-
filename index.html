About Cyber Pulse

A home for young builders, researchers and creators.

Cyber Pulse exists to bring ideas to life. The team combines research, product concepts, software development and experimentation to create technology designed around real student and community needs.

Rather than focusing on a single app, Cyber Pulse acts as an umbrella for multiple products and experiments, each with a dedicated purpose and people behind it.
